About War Party TOC

A TOC is a tactical operations center: the room where a unit keeps track of everything happening, all at once, so the right people know what matters. War Party TOC does that for the people who served and still serve: active duty, veterans, first responders, and their families.

Every day we sweep the news for the stories that affect pay, benefits, care, rights, and safety in our community. The Battle Rhythm is our daily rundown of what you need to know.

How it works

  • The Wire is gathered automatically. AI agents read hundreds of stories a day, sort them into four lanes, and post the relevant ones as headlines that link to the original reporting.
  • TOC Reports are ours. For the stories that matter most, the agents read the full reporting and draft a summary and a take. A veteran editor reviews, edits, and signs every TOC Report before it is published.
  • Our summaries are written in our own words and always link to the original story. We do not republish articles.
  • "Our take" is opinion and is labeled that way.
  • When we get something wrong, we pull it or correct it, and corrections are noted on the Report.
  • We report on research and policy. Nothing here is medical or legal advice.

The lanes

  • Active Duty: service members, military families, and military news from around the world.
  • Veterans: benefits, claims, care, toxic exposure, and transition.
  • First Responders: fire, police, EMS, and dispatch.
  • Integrative Health & Policy: research and law on psychedelic-assisted therapy, cannabis and hemp, and other integrative treatments, especially where veterans and responders are affected.

Who runs it

War Party TOC is a pilot from the founders of War Party, Inc., a veteran-focused advisory and advocacy firm. TOC Reports are edited by Will Wisner, an Army veteran of the Iraq war, and Anthony Canania.

Disclosures

  • Some of War Party, Inc.'s clients work in hemp, integrative health, and veteran health. When a story names one of them, it carries a disclosure, and those stories are always reviewed by an editor before they appear.
  • Our editors serve on the board of the Grunt Style Foundation. War Party TOC is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Foundation, and during the pilot we do not cover it.
  • AI assists with finding and drafting stories. People decide what is published.

Corrections

If we got something wrong, tell us and we will fix it and note the correction. Message the editors on our Facebook page or on X.

If you are struggling

Veterans Crisis Line: dial 988, then press 1. You can also text 838255. Help is available 24 hours a day.
