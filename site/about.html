About Battle Rhythm

In a headquarters, the battle rhythm is the daily cycle of updates that keeps everyone on the same page. Battle Rhythm does that for the people who served and still serve: active duty, veterans, first responders, and their families.

Every day we sweep the news for the stories that affect pay, benefits, care, rights, and safety in our community. We tell you what happened in plain language, why it matters, and what we think about it. Then we send you to the original reporting.

How it works

  • AI agents read hundreds of stories each day, sort them into four lanes, score them, and write a first draft.
  • A veteran editor reviews every item. Nothing is published until an editor edits and approves it, and the take carries that editor's name.
  • Our summaries are written in our own words and always link to the original story. We do not republish articles.
  • "Our take" is opinion and is labeled that way.
  • We report on research and policy. Nothing here is medical or legal advice.

The lanes

  • Active Duty: service members and military families.
  • Veterans / VA: benefits, claims, care, toxic exposure, and transition.
  • First Responders: fire, police, EMS, and dispatch.
  • Plant Medicine & Policy: research and law on psychedelic therapy, cannabis, and hemp, especially where veterans and responders are affected.

Who runs it

Battle Rhythm is a pilot edited by Will Wisner, an Army veteran of the Iraq war, and Anthony Canania.

Disclosures

  • Our editors are co-founders of War Party, Inc., a veteran-focused advisory and advocacy firm. Some of its clients work in hemp, plant medicine, and veteran health. When a story names one of them, it carries a disclosure.
  • Our editors serve on the board of the Grunt Style Foundation. Battle Rhythm is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Foundation, and during the pilot we do not cover it.
  • AI assists with finding and drafting stories. People decide what is published.

Corrections

If we got something wrong, tell us and we will fix it and note the correction. Message the editors on our Facebook page or on X.

If you are struggling

Veterans Crisis Line: dial 988, then press 1. You can also text 838255. Help is available 24 hours a day.
